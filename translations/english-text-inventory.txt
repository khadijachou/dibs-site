181 [faq,index] Where is my NIM while I save?
182 [faq,index] In your own Nimiq Pay wallet, staked under your address. Dibs only keeps track of which goal it belongs to.
183 [faq,index] Why is there a wait before I get my card?
184 [faq,index] Staked NIM takes 12 to 24 hours to unlock. That is a rule of the Nimiq network, not of Dibs. Once it is back, buying takes one tap.
185 [faq,index] Where do the gift cards come from?
186 [faq,index] Cards are bought from Bitrefill at checkout and paid over Lightning inside Nimiq Pay. Dibs is independent and not affiliated with Bitrefill or the brands in the catalog.
187 [faq,index] Does Dibs take a fee?
188 [index] No. You pay the shop price at checkout. The staking pool keeps its stated fee from rewards, which are not guaranteed.
189 [index] While you save, the staking pool keeps a small fee on the rewards it pays. That is the pool's fee, not Dibs's.
190 [faq,index] What if I change my mind?
191 [faq,index] Change the card, move your NIM to another goal, or take it back to your wallet. It is your NIM.

249 [safety] Staked from your own address
250 [safety] Your NIM is staked under your own address with the pool you choose from five checked options, Garuda Pool by default. Rewards are not guaranteed. Unstaking takes 12 to 24 hours.
251 [safety] No account, no email, no password. Your goals and your name stay on your phone.
252 [safety] Anonymous usage numbers are shared only if you tap Share stats, never with a name, a code or an address. You can switch it off or delete what was shared.
253 [safety] Money steps are never retried for you
254 [safety] Every notice says what happened, whether your NIM moved, and what to do next. Cancelling in Nimiq Pay is a normal path.
255 [safety] The whole app is public under the MIT licence. Anyone can read what Dibs does with each step.
256 [safety] Friends is
257 [safety] your choice
258 [safety] Friends is off until you turn it on. Your savings goals stay on your phone, and you control what your friends see.
259 [safety] Who sees what
260 [safety] Seven separate controls let you share nothing, share with friends, or share with everyone. Shared details are stored on the Friends server only while you choose to share them.
261 [safety] Private gift codes
262 [safety] Codes sent inside Dibs are locked on your phone for your friend's phone. The server cannot read them. They are deleted when opened or after 30 days.
263 [safety] Optional leaderboards

265 [safety] Your data to remove
266 [safety] The Friends server keeps the display name and gift information needed for optional features, including the message, banner and card value shown to the recipient. Remove Friends data from Profile > Friends > You.

311 [litepaper] 01 / 10
312 [litepaper] It starts with
313 [litepaper] "I want that."
314 [litepaper] Dibs was built around a small change in perspective: instead of asking people to save for the sake of saving, start with something they already care about.
315 [litepaper] Dibs began as a Nimiq Pay Mini App built around a small shift in motivation. The name comes from calling dibs: the moment you point to something and say, "I'm going for that." In Dibs, that decision becomes a goal, a plan and a visible path toward the finish line.
316 [litepaper] We want cryptocurrency to feel useful in everyday life. Not an abstract balance to watch, but something you can put toward a gift card, a meal, a trip or an experience. Dibs does not issue a token, run a token sale or ask you to hand over your keys.

396 [litepaper] CHAPTER 06
397 [litepaper] ECONOMICS
398 [litepaper] 06 / 10
399 [litepaper] A clear target.
400 [litepaper] Honest trade-offs.
401 [litepaper] Dibs does not charge a subscription or an additional Dibs fee for a gift-card purchase. That does not mean every part of the experience is free.
402 [litepaper] THE PRODUCT PRICE
403 [litepaper] Set by the shop
404 [litepaper] Bitrefill's payment price may be above the gift card's face value and can change.
405 [litepaper] THE SAVINGS TARGET
406 [litepaper] Shown in NIM
407 [litepaper] Computed using a live rate and a cushion for price movement. The final amount is checked at checkout.
408 [litepaper] STAKING REWARDS
409 [litepaper] Variable, not promised
410 [litepaper] Rewards depend on Nimiq staking and the validator. Pool fees affect the net amount received.
411 [litepaper] Because NIM has a market price, a target that looks sufficient today might not cover the same purchase tomorrow. Rewards do not remove price risk. If you're short at checkout, you may need to add more NIM; any extra NIM remains yours.
412 [litepaper] Not a guaranteed savings return.
413 [litepaper] Dibs is not a bank account or an investment product. Cryptocurrency prices can fall, staking rewards can vary, network fees may apply, and access to staked funds is not instant.

415 [litepaper] SAFETY & PRIVACY
416 [litepaper] 07 / 10
417 [litepaper] Your goal.
418 [litepaper] Your keys.
419 [litepaper] Our starting design choice is simple: Dibs should not need custody of your funds to help you save.
420 [litepaper] Display your goals and savings progress
421 [litepaper] Prepare actions for your approval in Nimiq Pay
422 [litepaper] Request catalog prices and coordinate a gift-card order
423 [litepaper] Dibs cannot
424 [litepaper] Spend NIM without your wallet approval
425 [litepaper] Access or store your wallet's private key
426 [litepaper] Guarantee rewards, exchange rates or product stock
427 [litepaper] Goal details are stored on the device in the current product. Anonymous usage statistics are optional: users choose whether to share them and can turn sharing off or delete shared records. 503 [faq] Where is my NIM staked?
504 [faq] You choose from five checked staking pools on your first deposit. Garuda Pool is selected by default. Each pool shows its fee and how often it pays. Your NIM stays under your own address, and rewards are not guaranteed.
505 [faq] Why does the first deposit need 100 NIM?
506 [faq] Nimiq requires 100 NIM to open a stake. After that first deposit you can add any amount, any week.
507 [faq] What if the price of NIM changes?
508 [faq] Your card has a price in its own currency. Dibs updates your goal in NIM at the live rate. The price is confirmed at checkout, so you might need more or less NIM.
509 [faq] No. Dibs adds no fee. You pay the shop price confirmed at checkout. The staking pool keeps the fee shown for its rewards. Rewards are not guaranteed.
510 [faq] Which version of a card should I pick?
511 [faq] Choose the version that works with your account. Some cards work in one place only, and some are for in-store use. Check the shop details before you call dibs.
512 [faq] What if the card is gone or a purchase fails?
513 [faq] Dibs checks the shop before you buy. If a card is out of stock or its price changed, you can change the card, keep the NIM for another goal, or take it back. If a payment does not go through, your NIM stays in your wallet, and Dibs never pays twice.
514 [faq] What does Dibs collect?
515 [faq] By default, goals stay on your phone and anonymous usage stats are off. If you enable Friends, its server keeps your display name and what you choose to share. Gift records and encrypted codes are handled as described in our Privacy page.
