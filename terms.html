This website

This site explains what Dibs is and how to open it in Nimiq Pay. It sells nothing and holds nothing. The numbers on it are measured facts on the dates shown and are updated by hand.

What Dibs does

You pick a gift card, add NIM from your own Nimiq Pay wallet, and Dibs stakes it with a Nimiq staking pool while you save. When the goal is reached, the NIM is released and pays for the card.

Your NIM

It stays in your own wallet the whole time. Staking rewards are not guaranteed. Releasing staked NIM takes 12 to 24 hours on the Nimiq network.

Prices

Prices in the app are shown in NIM from live rates. The final price is confirmed at checkout and can change. Any price on this site is an example.

Gift cards

Cards are sold and delivered by Bitrefill under its own terms. Dibs is independent and not affiliated with Bitrefill or the brands in the catalog. Brand names and logos belong to their owners.
