Privacy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This website collects nothing about you. Here is what that means, and what happens in the app.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. There is no form, no account and nothing to sign up for. The fonts and images are served from this site, so your browser makes no request to another company when the page loads. The numbers in the Proof section are public, anonymous totals: this site reads them from the Dibs stats server and passes them to the page.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel sees technical data such as your IP address when your browser asks for a page. We do not read or keep that data.

Links that leave this site

The demo video opens on YouTube. Transactions open on nimiq.watch. The code is on GitHub and our account is on X. The live totals open on the Dibs stats page. Each of those sites has its own privacy policy. Nothing loads from them until you tap the link.

The copy button

"Copy the app link" puts the app's address on your own clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere.

If you write to us

If you email calldibs.contact@gmail.com, we see your address and your message. We use them only to answer you.

The Dibs app

The app has its own privacy page, in More > Legal > Privacy. In short: your goals stay on your phone, Dibs never holds your NIM or your keys, and anonymous usage stats are shared only if you say yes.

What we never do

No ads, no trackers, nothing sold.

Call dibs on something

Dibs runs inside Nimiq Pay. Three steps and you are in.

  1. Open Nimiq Pay on your phone.
  2. Go to Mini Apps > Custom URL.
  3. Enter dibs-sepia.vercel.app or scan the code.
