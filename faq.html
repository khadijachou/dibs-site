Questions, answered

Short answers about saving, waiting and buying with Dibs.

Saving

Where is my NIM while I save?

In your own Nimiq Pay wallet, staked under your address. Dibs only keeps track of which goal it belongs to.

Where is my NIM staked?

With Garuda Pool, a Nimiq validator. Your NIM never leaves your account: the pool only validates with it. The pool adds rewards to your stake about every 12 hours, minus its own small fee. Rewards are not guaranteed.

Why does the first deposit need 100 NIM?

Nimiq requires 100 NIM to open a stake. After that first deposit you can add any amount, any week.

What if the price of NIM changes?

Your card keeps its price in its own currency. Dibs shows your target in NIM at the live rate, so the number you see stays current. At checkout you pay the card's price at that moment's rate: if NIM went up you need fewer, if it went down you add a little more.

Waiting and buying

Why is there a wait before I get my card?

Staked NIM takes 12 to 24 hours to unlock. That is a rule of the Nimiq network, not of Dibs. Once it is back, buying takes one tap.

Where do the gift cards come from?

Cards are bought from Bitrefill at checkout and paid over Lightning inside Nimiq Pay. Dibs is independent and not affiliated with Bitrefill or the brands in the catalog.

Does Dibs take a fee?

No. Dibs adds no fee. You pay the shop's own price for the card, which can be a little above its face value: a 5 USD card costs about 5.62 USD. Your savings target includes a 10% safety margin because the price of NIM moves, and the exact price is confirmed at checkout.

While you save, the staking pool keeps a small fee on the rewards it pays. That is the pool's fee, not Dibs's.

Which version of a card should I pick?

The one your account accepts. Each card says which accounts it works with: a USD Steam card works on any Steam wallet in USD, but a card made for one region may not work on an account from another. Picked the wrong one? Change the card from the goal: your NIM stays.

What if the card is gone or a purchase fails?

Dibs checks the shop before you buy. If a card is out of stock or its price changed, you can change the card, keep the NIM for another goal, or take it back. If a payment does not go through, your NIM stays in your wallet, and Dibs never pays twice.

Changes and your data

What if I change my mind?

Change the card, move your NIM to another goal, or take it back to your wallet. It is your NIM.

What does Dibs collect?

Nothing, unless you tap Share stats. Then it counts goals, deposits and cards anonymously: never your name, email or gift card code. You can switch it off or delete what was shared at any time. Privacy
