Your NIM stays yours

Dibs is a goal on top of your own wallet. It never holds your NIM and has no key to it. You approve every step in Nimiq Pay.

Dibs can

  • Show your goal and your progress
  • Prepare each step for you to approve in Nimiq Pay
  • Check the shop's price and stock before you buy
  • Show your gift card code in the app, on your phone

Dibs can't

  • Move your NIM without your approval
  • Hold your NIM or a key to your wallet
  • Pay twice for the same card
  • Keep your name, your goals or your codes on a server

How we keep it that way

Read the code on GitHub

  • Staked from your own address

    Saved NIM is staked under your own address with Garuda Pool. You can take it back whenever you want.

  • Nothing to sign up for

    No account, no email, no password. Your goals and your name stay on your phone.

  • Stats only if you say yes

    Anonymous usage numbers are shared only if you tap Share stats, never with a name, a code or an address. You can switch it off or delete what was shared.

  • Money steps are never retried for you

    Every notice says what happened, whether your NIM moved, and what to do next. Cancelling in Nimiq Pay is a normal path.

  • Open source

    The whole app is public under the MIT licence. Anyone can read what Dibs does with each step.

Gift cards by Bitrefill

Every brand and card in Dibs comes from Bitrefill, a gift card shop that takes Lightning payments. When your goal is complete, Dibs buys the card from Bitrefill at its own price, paid in NIM over Lightning inside Nimiq Pay.

Dibs is independent. It is not sponsored by, partnered with or endorsed by Bitrefill or any brand shown on this site.

Call dibs on something

Dibs runs inside Nimiq Pay. Three steps and you are in.

  1. Open Nimiq Pay on your phone.
  2. Go to Mini Apps > Custom URL.
  3. Enter dibs-sepia.vercel.app or scan the code.
