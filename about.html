The thing you called dibs on, ready to use.

Saving is easier when you can see what it is for. So Dibs starts with the thing itself: a game, a trip, a coffee run. You call dibs on it, save NIM toward it, and the NIM earns while you wait. When the goal is reached, one tap turns it into the card.

The story so far

  1. Late Oct 2026

    First version on testnet

    It started as Layaway. The name became Dibs before launch.

  2. 4 Nov 2026

    The first card, paid in NIM

    A Steam gift card paid from Nimiq Pay over Lightning. The code came back in about 8 seconds.

  3. 6 to 7 Nov 2026

    A full unlock on mainnet

    Stake deactivated, then released 12 h 4 min later. The wait the app promises, measured.

  4. 10 Nov 2026

    Dibs 1.0 goes live

    The whole path works with real NIM on Nimiq mainnet.

  5. 13 Nov 2026

    Dibs 1.1

    27 brands, Savings, History and Profile redesigned, and an even safer unlock.

  6. Today

    Dibs v1.1.13 is live

    A new release almost every day. See every release

What we hold to

  • No custody

    Your NIM never leaves your own wallet. No smart contract, no token of our own.

  • Honest waiting

    The 12 to 24 hour unlock is shown as it is, with the real release time and the transaction behind each step.

  • Privacy by default

    Goals and your name stay on your phone. No ads, no trackers. Stats only if you say yes.

  • Open by default

    MIT licence, public code, and a public page with the numbers.

Call dibs on something

Dibs runs inside Nimiq Pay. Three steps and you are in.

  1. Open Nimiq Pay on your phone.
  2. Go to Mini Apps > Custom URL.
  3. Enter dibs-sepia.vercel.app or scan the code.
