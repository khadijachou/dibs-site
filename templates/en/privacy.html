Privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Dibs keeps your goals on your phone. Friends, leaderboards and anonymous usage stats are optional. Here is what stays on the device, what leaves it, and why.

This website

No cookies, advertising, analytics or trackers. The images, scripts and fonts are served from this website. The public figures on Home are fetched through /api/stats from the Dibs stats server. Release history is read through /api/updates from the public app files on GitHub. No personal details are requested for either request.

Hosting

Vercel hosts this website and can receive routine technical data such as an IP address when your browser requests a page. Dibs does not use that data for advertising or analytics.

Links to other sites

You can choose to open the app, a demonstration video, the code repository, the chain explorer, the public stats page or our X account. Those services have their own privacy practices. Nothing from them loads just because you read this site.

Copying the app link

The Copy button puts the Dibs app address in your device's clipboard. It does not send a request.

If you write to us

Emailing calldibs.contact@gmail.com shares your email address and message with us. We use them to answer you.

In the app: on your phone

Your goals, settings, badges, XP and quests are stored on your phone. Your display name also stays on your phone unless you choose to enable Friends. Dibs does not send your name with usage statistics or purchases.

Your wallet

Dibs reads your NIM address and balance through Nimiq Pay. You approve deposits, staking steps and payments in Nimiq Pay. Dibs never holds your NIM or private keys.

Buying a gift card

Our server asks Bitrefill for a payment invoice and passes the email you type to Bitrefill for a receipt. Our server keeps the order number and a random access key, not your email address. Your code, PIN or link reaches your phone and is not stored on our server.

Usage statistics: only if you agree

If you tap Share stats, Dibs counts goals created, NIM deposited through Dibs and cards bought, plus total NIM staked and any rewards, anonymously with a random ID. Transaction IDs are checked against the chain once and then deleted. Never your name, email or codes. Turn sharing off or delete shared stats in Settings.

Friends: only if you turn it on

Friends keeps your display name, a random ID and friend code on a separate server. Your wallet signs once to link the profile; the server keeps a scrambled fingerprint of the wallet, not the address. You choose what to share, including level and rank, badges, cards bought, active goals and wishlist. You may also choose to share your Nimiq avatar, NIM saved and earned, and the value of goals or cards. Each control starts off and can be set to friends or everyone. Turning a setting off removes that detail from the server. Your wallet balance, wallet address and email are not shared through Friends.

Gift records and locked codes

Calling dibs on a friend's wish records which wish you chose and that you marked it sent, not the value. Gift messages, banners and card values are shown only to their recipient. Sending one of your own goals records who sent it, who received it and the card's brand.

A gift card code sent within Dibs is end-to-end encrypted on the sending phone so only the recipient's phone can open it. The Friends server relays it but cannot read it. Encrypted codes are deleted after opening or after 30 days.

Leaderboards: only if you join

Friends and global leaderboards are opt-in. If you join, the board uses your chosen profile name, level and checked XP. For deposit-related progress, Dibs checks a deposit on the Nimiq chain without storing its transaction hash or your wallet address. You can remove your Friends data in Profile > Friends > You.

Other services

Bitrefill sells and delivers gift cards under its own privacy policy. Nimiq's validators service loads the list of staking pools and may see technical data such as your IP address. Vercel and Render host the app and server, and the app uses Google Fonts. Those services may also receive technical data. This website serves its own fonts locally.

NIM rewards claimed outside the app

If you claim a promotional NIM reward by email, you may choose to send your Nimiq address so it can be paid. The app does not send that address for you. It is used only for that payment.

What we never do

No ads, no trackers, no data sales. Read the app's matching privacy text under More > Legal > Privacy.
