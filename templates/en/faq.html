Questions, answered

Short answers about saving, waiting and buying with Dibs.

Saving

Where is my NIM while I save?

In your own Nimiq Pay wallet, staked under your address. Dibs only keeps track of which goal it belongs to.

Where is my NIM staked?

You choose from five checked staking pools on your first deposit. Garuda Pool is selected by default. Each pool shows its fee and how often it pays. Your NIM stays under your own address, and rewards are not guaranteed.

Why does the first deposit need 100 NIM?

Nimiq requires 100 NIM to open a stake. After that first deposit you can add any amount, any week.

What if the price of NIM changes?

Your card has a price in its own currency. Dibs updates your goal in NIM at the live rate. The price is confirmed at checkout, so you might need more or less NIM.

Waiting and buying

Why is there a wait before I get my card?

Staked NIM takes 12 to 24 hours to unlock. That is a rule of the Nimiq network, not of Dibs. Once it is back, buying takes one tap.

Where do the gift cards come from?

Cards are bought from Bitrefill at checkout and paid over Lightning inside Nimiq Pay. Dibs is independent and not affiliated with Bitrefill or the brands in the catalog.

Does Dibs take a fee?

No. Dibs adds no fee. You pay the shop price confirmed at checkout. The staking pool keeps the fee shown for its rewards. Rewards are not guaranteed.

Which version of a card should I pick?

Choose the version that works with your account. Some cards work in one place only, and some are for in-store use. Check the shop details before you call dibs.

What if the card is gone or a purchase fails?

Dibs checks the shop before you buy. If a card is out of stock or its price changed, you can change the card, keep the NIM for another goal, or take it back. If a payment does not go through, your NIM stays in your wallet, and Dibs never pays twice.

Changes and your data

What if I change my mind?

Change the card, move your NIM to another goal, or take it back to your wallet. It is your NIM.

What does Dibs collect?

By default, goals stay on your phone and anonymous usage stats are off. If you enable Friends, its server keeps your display name and what you choose to share. Gift records and encrypted codes are handled as described in our Privacy page.

Friends and gifts

Can I choose where my NIM is staked?+

Yes. On your first deposit, choose among five checked pools. Dibs shows each pool's fee and reward frequency. Garuda Pool is the default. Rewards are not guaranteed.

Can I send a gift to a friend?+

Yes. With optional Friends enabled, you can call dibs on a friend's shared wish or gift one of your own goals. Add a message and banner; your friend sees a full-screen reveal in Dibs.

Can Dibs see the gift code I send?+

No. A code sent inside Dibs is end-to-end encrypted on your phone for your friend's phone. The Friends server only relays the locked code. It is deleted once opened or after 30 days.

Do I have to join Friends or the leaderboards?+

No. Both are optional. Friends is off until you turn it on, and joining a leaderboard is a separate choice. Deposits counted toward leaderboards are checked on the chain without storing the hash or wallet address.

What does a friend see about me?+

Only what you choose to share. Seven controls let you set each detail to off, friends or everyone. You can check your profile with View as and remove your Friends data in Profile > Friends > You.
