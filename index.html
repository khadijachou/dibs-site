Four steps, one goal

Pick a card, add NIM from your wallet, let it earn while you save, then unlock and get your card. Staking rewards are not guaranteed.

  1. 01

    Call dibs

    Choose a gift card, its value and a date. Make the thing you want the goal.

  2. 02

    Save up

    Approve each deposit in Nimiq Pay. On the first deposit, choose among five checked staking pools. See where your NIM takes the goal.

  3. 03

    It earns while you wait

    Your pool adds rewards about every 12 hours, minus its fee. Rewards are not guaranteed. See the next reward time on Savings.

  4. 04

    It's yours

    At 100%, unlock and wait 12 to 24 hours for the network to release your NIM. Then pay with one tap over Lightning. Your code appears in Dibs.

How gifting works →

Your NIM never leaves your hands

Dibs is a goal on top of your own wallet. It never holds your NIM and has no key to it. You approve every step in Nimiq Pay.

  • Staked from your wallet

    Each deposit is staked under your own address. You can request your NIM back, subject to the network's 12 to 24 hour release wait. Rewards are not guaranteed.

  • Nothing to sign up for

    No account, no email, no password. Your goals stay on your phone.

  • Stats only if you say yes

    Anonymous usage numbers are shared only with your consent, never with a name, a code or an address.

Questions

Something else? Write to calldibs.contact@gmail.com

Where is my NIM while I save?

In your own Nimiq Pay wallet, staked under your address. Dibs only keeps track of which goal it belongs to.

Why is there a wait before I get my card?

Staked NIM takes 12 to 24 hours to unlock. That is a rule of the Nimiq network, not of Dibs. Once it is back, buying takes one tap.

Where do the gift cards come from?

Cards are bought from Bitrefill at checkout and paid over Lightning inside Nimiq Pay. Dibs is independent and not affiliated with Bitrefill or the brands in the catalog.

Does Dibs take a fee?

No. You pay the shop price at checkout. The staking pool keeps its stated fee from rewards, which are not guaranteed.

While you save, the staking pool keeps a small fee on the rewards it pays. That is the pool's fee, not Dibs's.

What if I change my mind?

Change the card, move your NIM to another goal, or take it back to your wallet. It is your NIM.
