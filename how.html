How Dibs works

Four moves, one wait. Here is what happens at each step, and what happens to your NIM along the way.

  1. Call dibsPick a gift card from 27 brands, choose the value and a date. That is your goal.

  2. Save up, your wayAdd a little each week, or all at once. Your NIM is staked from your own wallet through Nimiq staking.

What happens to your NIM

Follow your NIM

From your first deposit to your card, the NIM stays in your own account until the moment you pay.

Always under your own address
  1. In your wallet

    Your NIM sits in Nimiq Pay, under your own address.

  2. Staked

    You approve each deposit. It is staked with Garuda Pool, a Nimiq validator.

  3. Earning

    Rewards land about every 12 hours, minus the pool's small fee. Not guaranteed.

  4. Unlocked

    At 100%, you stop staking. The network releases it 12 to 24 hours later.

  5. Paid

    One tap pays the shop over Lightning. The code arrives in the app.

A goal, in weekly steps

Dibs prices your card in NIM at the live rate, adds a 10% cushion because the price of NIM moves, and splits the target into weekly steps.

Nothing is automatic. Skip a week and catch up the next one. The first deposit needs 100 NIM to open a stake, then any amount works.

One wait, then one tap

When you unlock, the Nimiq network releases staked NIM after 12 to 24 hours. That is a network rule, not a Dibs one. Dibs shows you a clock with the real release time.

Once it is back, getting the card is one tap.

What it costs

Dibs adds no fee. You pay the shop's own price for the card, which can be a little above its face value. The exact price is confirmed at checkout.
