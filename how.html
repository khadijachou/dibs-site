How Dibs works

Four moves, one wait. Here is what happens at each step, and what happens to your NIM along the way.

Open in Nimiq PayTry the demoIn your browser, no wallet needed

  1. Call dibs Pick a gift card, its value and a date. Choose from 33 brands.

  2. Save up Approve each deposit in Nimiq Pay. Your first deposit lets you choose from five checked staking pools; Garuda Pool is the default. The Add NIM screen previews where your deposit takes the goal.

  3. It earns while you wait Your staking pool adds rewards about every 12 hours, minus its fee. Rewards are not guaranteed. Savings shows the next reward time.

  4. It's yours At 100%, unlock your NIM. The network releases it after 12 to 24 hours. Dibs estimates when it comes back. Then pay with one tap over Lightning and receive the card in Dibs.

What happens to your NIM

Follow your NIM

From your first deposit to your card, the NIM stays in your own account until the moment you pay.

Always under your own address
  1. In your wallet

    Your NIM sits in Nimiq Pay, under your own address.

  2. Staked

    On your first deposit, choose among five checked pools. Garuda Pool is the default. You approve each staking transaction.

  3. Earning

    Your pool adds rewards about every 12 hours, minus its stated fee. Rewards are not guaranteed.

  4. Unlocked

    At 100%, you stop staking. The network releases it 12 to 24 hours later.

  5. Paid

    One tap pays the shop over Lightning. The code arrives in the app.

A goal, in weekly steps

Dibs converts the shop price into a NIM goal at the live rate, adds a 10% cushion for price changes, and divides the target into weekly steps.

Nothing is automatic. Skip a week and catch up the next one. The first deposit needs 100 NIM to open a stake, then any amount works.

One wait, then one tap

When you unlock, the Nimiq network releases staked NIM after 12 to 24 hours. That is a network rule, not a Dibs one. Dibs shows you a clock with the real release time.

Once it is back, getting the card is one tap.

What it costs

Dibs adds no fee. You pay the shop's own price for the card, which can be a little above its face value. The exact price is confirmed at checkout.

Gifts, your way

Friends and gifting are optional. Your goals and NIM remain under your control.

Save for a friend's wish

See a friend's shared wishlist, call dibs on a card and save toward it in your own wallet. They are not told until you send the gift.

Gift one of your goals

Choose a goal you have already saved for and give its card to a friend. Add a personal message and a banner.

A reveal just for them

The gift opens full screen in Dibs. Your friend can choose Say thanks. The card's value is shown to its recipient.

A code only they can open

Send the card code inside Dibs with end-to-end encryption. Only your friend's phone can open it; Dibs cannot read it. See Delivered and Opened, with unread codes deleted after 30 days.
